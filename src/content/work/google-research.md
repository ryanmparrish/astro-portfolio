## Google Research

From February to May 2026, I worked with a small development team at Instrument to bring Google Research's updated brand guidelines into its web experience.

### Modular CMS components

Working within a Django and Wagtail platform, I built additional CMS components and translated design system guidance into reusable UI. The work focused on performance, accessibility, and editorial workflows.

### Cross-functional collaboration

I partnered with design, backend, and content teams during planning and implementation. I also contributed code reviews and mentorship as the team developed a more modular UI system.
